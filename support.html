Common questions

Does the app send messages by itself? No. It opens WhatsApp with the message ready; you check it and tap send.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. No account, no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → your picture → Payments & subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

New phone, or reinstalled the app? In the app's Settings, tap "استرجع اشتراكي" (restore my subscription).
